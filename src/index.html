How to play

1 · Build

Pick a part, then tap the deck to place it. Every block has mass and buoyancy: if the ship weighs more than it floats, it won't launch. Every block must connect to the single Bridge. Speed comes from engines against weight; turning from rudders.

2 · Fight

Turrets aim and fire by themselves — you steer. Keyboard: W/S throttle, A/D rudder, Tab or click to focus fire, F smoke, C camera, drag to look. Touch: drag the left half to steer and set throttle, drag the right half to look, tap an enemy to focus fire.

3 · Survive

Shells hit the block they land on. Lost blocks cost buoyancy and the ship lists toward the damage. Lose the bridge, or too much buoyancy, and you sink. Fires spread until they burn out — Repair Bays put them out.

4 · Progress

Battles pay credits. Spend them in the Armory on new parts, engineering upgrades and a bigger dock. Each operation has three stars: win, finish with 60% of your hull, and beat the par time. Harder difficulty pays more.
