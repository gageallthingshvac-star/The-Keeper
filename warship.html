IRON TIDE

Design a warship block by block. Send it to sea. Watch it come apart.
Operations
Shipyard. Every block has mass and buoyancy. If the ship weighs more than it floats, it sinks — in the yard or at sea. Blocks must connect to the Bridge; lose the bridge and the ship is lost. Pieces cut off from the bridge break away.
At sea. Weapons aim and fire on their own. You steer. W/S throttle, A/D rudder, Tab or click/tap an enemy to focus fire, mouse wheel zoom. On touch: drag on the left half of the screen to steer and set throttle.
Damage. Shells hit the block they land on. Armor soaks hits. Every lost block costs buoyancy — take enough holes and the sea finishes the job.
