## Trees (public/assets/trees)
* All tree assets are original work generated by `tools/blender/trees.py` (Blender 5.2 Cycles + numpy). No third-party textures or models were downloaded.
  Leaf / frond / fan cards are Cycles renders of procedural 3D leaf geometry; bark textures are periodic procedural height fields; LOD0/LOD1 `.glb` meshes come from space-colonisation skeletons; impostor atlases are Cycles renders of those meshes. Same license as the project code.
