Hotdog Flip

Dogs drop onto the grill. Only the side touching the grate cooks.

  • Tap a dog to flip it.
  • When both sides are golden, tap it again to serve.
  • Hit 70–85% on a side for a perfect bonus.
  • Let the underside reach 100% and it burns. Three burns ends your shift.
