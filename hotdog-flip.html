Hotdog Flip

A dog flies in from the left and lands in your pan. Launch it onto whatever is sitting on the counter.

  • Hold the button (or Space) to charge. The meter swings up and down.
  • Release to snap the pan up and flip the dog.
  • It has to land flat and on top. Tilted landings bounce off.
  • Targets get smaller, start moving, and wind picks up. Three drops and you're out.
